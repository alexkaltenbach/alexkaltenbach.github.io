## Research Projects

<div class="funding-entry funding-entry--with-logo funding-entry--project">
  <div class="funding-entry__body">
    <h3>Learning-Enhanced Robust Iterative Methods for Non-Smooth and Constrained Variational Problems</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Acronym:</strong> LenhTVHM</li>
      <li><strong>Funding line:</strong> DFG-NSFC Sino-German Call on Intelligent Numerical Mathematics (NSFC-DFG 2025 iNum)</li>
      <li><strong>Project duration:</strong> 2027-2029</li>
      <li><strong>Funding awarded:</strong> €201,800 plus 22% programme allowance (DFG share)</li>
      <li><strong>PIs:</strong> Sören Bartels, Yi Zhu, Alex Kaltenbach, and Shuo Yang</li>
      <li><strong>Project page:</strong> <a href="https://gepris.dfg.de/project/581203342">DFG GEPRIS</a></li>
    </ul>
    <p>This project develops robust iterative methods for non-smooth and constrained variational problems by combining classical numerical analysis with machine-learning components. It focuses on adaptive parameter selection, improved initial iterates, and automated solver strategies, with the aim of increasing reliability and computational efficiency for challenging variational models in mathematics and physics.</p>
  </div>
  <img class="funding-logo funding-logo--wide" src="/images/funding/DFG-logo.png" alt="DFG logo">
</div>

<div class="funding-entry funding-entry--with-logo funding-entry--project">
  <div class="funding-entry__body">
    <h3>DOC-TWIN: Duality-Based Error Control for Optimal Control Problems in Digital-Twin-Based Structural Health Monitoring</h3>
    <ul>
      <li><strong>Funding line:</strong> MATH+ Application Area Technology, project AA-Tech-4</li>
      <li><strong>Project duration:</strong> 2027-2028</li>
      <li><strong>Funding awarded:</strong> one full-time research position (TV-L E13, 100%) for two years</li>
      <li><strong>PIs:</strong> Michael Hintermüller, Alex Kaltenbach, and Harbir Antil</li>
      <li><strong>Project page:</strong> <a href="https://mathplus.de/research-2/application-areas/aa2-nano-quantum-technologies/application-areas-aa-tech-4/">MATH+ AA-Tech-4</a></li>
    </ul>
    <p>This project develops a (discrete) convex-duality-based framework for error control of optimal control problems in digital twins for structural health monitoring of civil infrastructure. It aims to derive new a posteriori and a priori error identities that enable adaptive mesh refinement, rigorous error control, and certified nonlinear solvers for reliability-critical simulations.</p>
  </div>
