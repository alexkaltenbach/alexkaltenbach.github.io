Research source

A Finite Element Approximation of an Optimal Insulation Problem with
Convective Heat Transfer.

Section 7.1, manufactured test case; Figure 2 displays FE approximations

Content
Blue shading shows the exact manufactured temperature in the unit square.
White contours are levels 0.75, 1.5, 2.25, 3.0 of that same exact function.
Orange width on each flat edge is proportional to the exact optimal
normal allocation. The top and bottom edges are uncoated on
[0.3765,0.6184]; the vertical sides are coated throughout.
The exterior width and rounded corner joins are explicitly schematic,
as indicated inside the image. No finite-thickness optimum is claimed.

Validation
The exact boundary integral used for normalization is
I=0.39763866666666664; omega=m*beta/I=15.089075844401451.
The analytic mass equals m=4. Independent 100001-point trapezoidal
integration on each side returns 4.0 to floating-point accuracy.
Temperature range on the rendering grid: 0.375 to 3.142967813251474.
Maximum optimal normal allocation: 1.8358375610688433.
FORMULAS.txt records the complete benchmark and exact normalization.
