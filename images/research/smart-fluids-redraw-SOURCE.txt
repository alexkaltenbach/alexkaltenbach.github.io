SMART-FLUID BENCHMARK — NEW WEB VISUAL

Use smart-fluids-profile-new.svg for the website. The PNG is a high-resolution
preview. The two panels share the same horizontal coordinates.

The upper band identifies p(x)=2.5 for -1<=x<=0.5 and p(x)=1.5 for 0.5<x<=1.
The lower panel shows the exact stationary velocity profile and actual finite
element approximations on 4 and 32 cells. The original experiment refines
space and time together: 4 and 32 time steps, respectively. Curves are
final-time values. This is a stationary benchmark in
the public pulsatile-flow article, not an observed physical flow or a new
time-dependent numerical experiment.

Numerical profiles are extracted from PDF vector paths in the author's
Figure 9, left panel. No values have been invented, fitted to a convergence
rate, or produced by a new solver run. PDF display coordinates have finite
precision: they are suitable for faithful visualization, not for new
quantitative error estimates. Axis-calibration residuals are below 3.8e-8 in
data coordinates; the recomputed published exact formula agrees with the
original exact-curve path to 1.42e-7 at its vertices.

The original nine approximation curves are reduced to two representative
levels. The flux line is omitted to keep the comparison legible. Styles,
layout and explanatory labels are new; the numerical curve values are kept.

CAPTION
A discontinuous material exponent produces an asymmetric velocity profile.
The finite element approximations on 4 and 32 cells are compared with the
exact stationary benchmark.
