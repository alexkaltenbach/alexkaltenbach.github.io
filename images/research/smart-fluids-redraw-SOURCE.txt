The numerical profiles have 4 cells / 4 time steps and 32 cells / 32 time
steps. They are the author's actual final-time curves for a stationary exact
benchmark, extracted from the PDF vector paths. They are rounded plotted data,
not fresh raw solver results. See provenance.json for calibration errors and
the complete source hash. No new convergence claims should be made.

The exact curve is recomputed from the published piecewise formula and
validated against the paper curve. It is not a fitted curve.

Visual changes: two refinements rather than nine; separate red exponent axis;
STIX serif mathematics; restrained grid; no decorative band or area fill.
