What the curves show
The squared primal-dual gap estimator against degrees of freedom for the
scalar Signorini problem on (-1,1)^2 with mixed boundary conditions.
Adaptive refinement: theta=1/2, 21 levels.
Uniform refinement: theta=1, 5 levels.
These are estimator values, not a measured exact-error norm. The exact
solution is unknown in this experiment.

Extraction and accuracy
All plotted samples were recovered from the original PDF vector polylines,
not from raster pixels and not from a newly run solver. The blue polyline
contains 21 samples; the green polyline contains 5. Every recovered vertex
was checked against the center of its corresponding original marker.
Maximum vertex/marker discrepancy: approximately 0.000031 PDF point.

The logarithmic axis transformations were calibrated using the exact PDF
positions of the major grid lines and their text labels (x: 10^2,10^3,10^4;
y: 10^-1,10^-2,10^-3,10^-4). Calibration residuals and transformations are
saved in signorini-vector-data.json. The original PDF coordinate precision
limits recovered numerical values. They are approximate vector-derived
values, not original full-precision solver data; the many CSV digits preserve
the reproducible extraction, not a claim of simulation precision.
Degrees of freedom recover to within about 0.002 of their integer values.
No data point was added, fitted, smoothed, or removed. Lines connect the
original samples on the same logarithmic scales.

Visual changes
White background; reduced grid; sans-serif labels; blue circles for adaptive
and orange open squares for uniform refinement; clear direct labels;
shortened x-axis label and explicit y-axis label 'Squared gap estimator'.
The original slope triangles, legends and auxiliary mathematical notation
were omitted for legibility. No numerical efficiency badge is asserted.

Suggested caption
Refinement guided by the primal-dual gap yields faster estimator decay than
uniform refinement in this scalar Signorini example. Redrawn from Figure 4
in joint work with Sören Bartels and Thirupathi Gudi.

Suggested alt text
Logarithmic plot of the squared primal-dual gap estimator against degrees
of freedom. The adaptive curve in blue decreases more steeply and reaches
a smaller value than the uniform-refinement curve in orange.
