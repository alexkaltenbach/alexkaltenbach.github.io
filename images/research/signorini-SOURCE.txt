WHAT IT SHOWS
For a scalar Signorini problem with unknown exact solution on (-1,1)^2 and
mixed boundary conditions, the squared primal-dual gap estimator is compared
against the number of degrees of freedom. Blue circles: adaptive refinement
(theta=1/2, 21 levels). Green squares: uniform refinement (theta=1, 5 levels).
The displayed reference slopes are N^(-1) for adaptive and N^(-2/3) for uniform
refinement. This is an estimator plot, not a measured exact-error norm.

SUGGESTED CAPTION
Refinement guided by the primal-dual gap yields faster decay of the squared
gap estimator than uniform refinement for this scalar Signorini problem.
Figure 4 in joint work with Sören Bartels and Thirupathi Gudi.

ALT TEXT
Log-log comparison of the squared primal-dual gap estimator against degrees
of freedom for the scalar Signorini problem. Blue circles show adaptive
refinement with reference rate N to the power minus one; green squares show
uniform refinement with reference rate N to the power minus two thirds.
