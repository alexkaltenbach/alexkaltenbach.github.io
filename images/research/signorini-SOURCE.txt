SIGNORINI: ORIGINAL PAPER COLORS, PUBLICATION-STYLE REDRAW

Research source: Sören Bartels, Thirupathi Gudi, Alex Kaltenbach,
A priori and a posteriori error identities for the scalar Signorini problem.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.10912v1
Figure4(right), p.23; author-uploaded images/aposteriori_rates.pdf.
Published DOI: https://doi.org/10.1137/24M1677691

All 21 adaptive and 5 uniform samples are unchanged from the validated
PDF vector extraction in signorini-vector-data.json/.csv. Polylines were
calibrated from labelled logarithmic major grid lines and checked against
marker centers (maximum discrepancy 0.000031 PDF point). These are approximate
vector-derived values, not original full-precision solver data. No samples
are added, fitted, smoothed or removed.

Exact original series colors restored:
adaptive circles: #1f77b4 (Matplotlib tab:blue)
uniform squares: #2ca02c (Matplotlib tab:green)

The true quantity is the squared primal-dual gap estimator, not a measured
exact-error norm. Grey dashed segments show reference rates N^(-1) and
N^(-2/3), already indicated in the source figure. They are not fitted curves.
STIX serif typography, restrained scientific legend and moderate grid replace
the previous web-themed direct-label style. No generic title is embedded.
