NEURAL APPROXIMATION OF PARAMETRIC PDES — EXACT REFERENCE VISUAL

Use neural-parametric-map.svg on the website; PNG is a fallback.
The heatmap is a new rendering of the exact family in Section 8.1 of:
Alex Kaltenbach and Marius Zeinhofer,
The Deep Ritz Method for parametric p-Dirichlet problems,
Advances in Continuous and Discrete Models 2026, article 8.
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13662-025-04043-2

Formula:
u(mu,x) = (sin(mu*pi*x) - sin(mu*pi)*x) / pi**2
for x in (-1,1), mu in (0,6). The closed boundary is displayed by continuity.
This solves -u_xx = mu**2*sin(mu*pi*x), with zero endpoint values.
The parameter mu is the forcing parameter, not a neural-network weight.

The visual shows the analytical benchmark. It does not show newly trained
network predictions or reproduce the paper's numerical errors. The article
reports no public numerical code. No training or solver is needed to redraw it.

Reproduce with Python 3, NumPy and Matplotlib:
  python plot_neural_family.py
The script exports only the chosen SVG and PNG and prints validation results.
The formula and sampling settings replace any need for a large data archive.
Exact settings and proposed caption/alternative text are in provenance.json.
