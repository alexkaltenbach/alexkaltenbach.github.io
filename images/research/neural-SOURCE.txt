Source: Alex Kaltenbach and Marius Zeinhofer,
The Deep Ritz Method for parametric p-Dirichlet problems,
Advances in Continuous and Discrete Models 2026, article 8, Section 8.1.
https://doi.org/10.1186/s13662-025-04043-2
https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.01894

Exact formula:
u(mu,x) = (sin(mu*pi*x) - x*sin(mu*pi)) / pi**2
for x in (-1,1), mu in (0,6), extended continuously to the displayed boundary.
It satisfies -u_xx = mu**2*sin(mu*pi*x), u(mu,-1)=u(mu,1)=0.
mu is the forcing parameter; u* denotes the exact solution.
